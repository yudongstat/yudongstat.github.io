About

I am an Associate Professor in the Faculty of Business Administration at the University of Macau. My research interests include distributed inference, semiparametric inference, and transfer learning. My work develops statistically principled and computationally efficient methods for modern data settings, with particular interests in distributed and heterogeneous data, semiparametric models, and applications in healthcare and reliability.

Research

Research Interests

Distributed Inference

Statistical inference and learning across distributed and heterogeneous data sources, with an emphasis on communication-efficient procedures and one-shot aggregation.

Semiparametric Inference

Flexible semiparametric modeling and computation, including sieve estimation, survival analysis, censoring, and shape-constrained problems.

Transfer Learning

Statistical methodology for borrowing information across related data sources while accommodating heterogeneity between populations or study sites.
