About Me

I am an Associate Professor in Business Intelligence and Analytics (Statistics) at the Faculty of Business Administration, University of Macau. Before joining the University of Macau, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher in the PENNCIL lab at the University of Pennsylvania, and a Research Fellow in the ISEM Department at the National University of Singapore. I received my PhD in Industrial Engineering from the National University of Singapore in 2023, under the supervision of Professor Zhisheng Ye.

Research Interests

  • Distributed inference
  • Semiparametric inference
  • Statistical computing
  • Operations management
